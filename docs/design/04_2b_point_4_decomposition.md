## 4. THE FREEZE

### 4.1 THE DEFINITION

> ### 4.7 IS THE FREEZE. IT IS THE COMMIT AFTER WHICH THE VALIDATION DESIGN IS
> ### CLOSED AND BEFORE WHICH THE ENGINE HAS NEVER BEEN RUN ON REAL DATA IN FULL
> ### EVALUATION MODE.

**WHAT MUST BE COMMITTED BEFORE IT:** every document 4.0 through 4.6 names,
including this one, and the register at §5 fully dispositioned.

**WHAT MAY NOT BE DONE BEFORE IT:** the engine may not be run in full evaluation
mode on real data, and no outcome quantity may be computed, inspected or
estimated. `docs/handoff/31_point_5_closing.md` §10 states this and §11 records
that the engine has been **deliberately left unrun**.

**WHAT BECOMES PERMISSIBLE AFTER IT AND WAS NOT BEFORE:** exactly one thing.

### 4.2 THE ONE THING IT UNLOCKS

> ### AFTER THE FREEZE THE ENGINE MAY BE RUN IN FULL EVALUATION MODE ON THE
> ### IN-SAMPLE WINDOW. THAT RUN COMPUTES OUTCOME QUANTITIES FOR THE FIRST TIME IN
> ### THIS THESIS'S LIFE.

**THE FIREWALL LIFTS AT THE FREEZE AND NOT BEFORE.**

> ### LIFTING IT IS IRREVERSIBLE. Once a figure has been seen it cannot be
> ### unseen, and every subsequent design choice is made by someone who has seen
> ### it.

**THAT IS THE WHOLE REASON THE PRECEDING SIX SUB-POINTS EXIST.** They are not
preparation for the run; they are the things that stop being decidable the moment
it happens.

**THE HOLDOUT DOES NOT UNLOCK AT 4.7.** It remains sealed, on the budget
`docs/handoff/31_point_5_closing.md` §14 states: one candidate, one look, whole
window, no candidate two. **The freeze unlocks the in-sample window only.**

### 4.3 THE PRECONDITIONS — A CHECKLIST A READER CAN EVALUATE

**EACH IS EITHER MET OR NOT, AND A READER CAN TELL WHICH.**

1. **EVERY SUB-POINT THROUGH 4.6 IS COMMITTED**, and each of §9(a) to (f) has a
   document that discharges it.
2. **§9(g) IS DISCHARGED**: all nine items at
   `docs/handoff/31_point_5_closing.md` §5 carry a decision or an explicit
   deferral naming what they are deferred to.
3. **THE SPECIFICATION AND THE IMPLEMENTATION DO NOT DIVERGE IN ANY KNOWN
   RESPECT.** **One divergence is known and open today:**
   `docs/design/04_1g_cap_adoption.md` §5 records that `src/engine/simulate.py`
   still applies a stop cap the specification has retired. **It must be closed or
   §4.4's third option invoked.**
4. **THE MECHANICAL DEBT AT `docs/design/04_2a_artifact_containment.md` §7 IS
   CLEARED, OR EXPLICITLY DEFERRED WITH REASONS.** §5.1 below assigns it.
5. **THE CONTAINMENT GUARD IS IN FORCE** — the tree-wide read guard
   `docs/design/04_2a_artifact_containment.md` §3.6 commits, not merely the
   partial guard already committed with report 41.
6. **THE FIXTURE CARVE-OUT IS RECORDED IN SOURCE** on the model
   `docs/design/04_2a_artifact_containment.md` §4.4 names, with its four
   conditions asserted by test.

### 4.4 IF A PRECONDITION IS UNMET WHEN THE FREEZE IS WANTED

> ### THE HONEST OPTIONS ARE TWO: MEET IT, OR DEFER THE FREEZE.

**A THIRD IS AVAILABLE AND IS NARROWLY CONDITIONED.** Freezing with a known
divergence and recording it is permitted **only if both of the following appear in
the freeze document itself:**

- **THE DIVERGENCE, STATED** — what the specification says and what the
  implementation does.
- **ITS EFFECT ON THE RUN, STATED** — what the figures the run produces will and
  will not describe, given the divergence.

> ### A DIVERGENCE RECORDED SOMEWHERE ELSE AND CITED FROM THE FREEZE DOCUMENT DOES
> ### NOT MEET THIS. The freeze document is where a reader looks to learn what the
> ### run means, and a reference is not a statement.

**AND THE THIRD OPTION IS NOT A GENERAL LICENCE.** It exists because deferring
indefinitely is also a failure mode, and because a divergence whose effect is
stated is survivable in a way that one discovered afterwards is not.
